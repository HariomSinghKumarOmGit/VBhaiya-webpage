CHAPTER III

Sri Yantra Sadhana

A graduated nine-month journey through the Avarans of the Sri Chakra — invoking Lalita Tripura Sundari layer by layer.

Avaran 1 — Trailokya Mohana Chakra (Month 1) ⌄
1st Avaran · Bhupura
Trailokya Mohana — Enchanter of the Three Worlds
Objective Activate the Third Eye (Ajna Chakra) & awaken devotion to Lalita Tripura Sundari.

Main Practice

  1. Tratak + Lalita Sahasranam Meditation — 30–45 min on the 1st Avaran. Apply a small paste-dot at the centre with the right ring finger. Play the audio while doing mansik jaap of the names.
  2. Mansik Jaap — silently chant your Source Mantra in Gyan Mudra (thumb & index finger touching).

Energy Alignment

  • Visualize Devi's bindu between the brows radiating light.
  • Bhavana: Surrender fully to Devi's inner vision.
  • Light attar: Sukhad (Kesar + Chandan), Zafran, Amber, Musk, or your favourite — pure, not expensive.

Paste for Sri Yantra Dot

  • Kesar — 1 pinch
  • Chandan powder — 1 pinch
  • Sugar — 1 pinch
  • Camphor — 1 pinch
  • Ganga Jal or Rose water

Spiritual Sadhana Oil (Lamp)

  • Kesar — 1 pinch
  • Chandan — 1 pinch
  • Sugar — 1 pinch
  • Camphor — 1 pinch
  • 1 small edible gold leaf
  • Cold-pressed Til oil — 1 litre

Mix all in a clean bottle; let sit 24 hrs. Light your lamp daily as a subtle offering to Devi.

Daily Guidelines
  • Practice at the same time daily.
  • Okay to skip during travel.
  • Women: skip during periods; resume after a bath.
Avaran 2 — Sarva Aasha Paripuraka Chakra ⌄
2nd Avaran · 16-Petal Lotus
Sarva Aasha Paripuraka — Fulfiller of All Desires
Bhavana Receiving wish-fulfilling blessings through Devi's presence.

Practice Flow

  • Tratak on the 2nd Avaran (Sarva Aasha Paripuraka Chakra) — 30 minutes.
  • Mark the centre of the second Avaran on a printed Sri Yantra. Apply a gram-sized dot with the ring finger of the right hand, dipped in Khus paste.
  • Mantra: Source Mantra — 30 minutes mansik jaap.
  • Mudra: Gyan Mudra.

Tilak Paste

  • Khus root powder mixed with a few drops of water to form a paste.
  • Optional: a drop of rose water or gangajal for cooling effect.

Lamp Oil

  • To regular lamp oil add ½ spoon Khus root powder.
  • Few drops (~5 ml) of Heena (Mehendi) attar or Khus attar.

Body Application

  • Apply Heena attar on both palms, rub gently together.
  • Place hands on the Heart Chakra as an offering gesture.
Avaran 3 — Sarva Sankshobhana Chakra (60 min) ⌄
3rd Avaran · 8-Petal Lotus
Sarva Sankshobhana — Agitator of All
Bhavana Stirring and awakening the inner spiritual energy through Devi's presence.

Practice Flow

  • Tratak on the 3rd Avaran — 30 minutes.
  • Mantra: Source Mantra — 60 minutes mansik jaap.
  • Mudra: Gyan Mudra / Vishuddha (Throat Chakra) Mudra.

Tilak Paste

  • Chandan powder + crushed pearls (Moti) + a trace of silver leaf.
  • A few drops of water to form a thick paste.
  • Optional: a drop of White Oudh attar for sacred aroma.

Lamp Oil

  • ½ spoon Chandan powder
  • A trace of crushed pearls
  • A bit of silver leaf
  • ~5 ml White Oudh attar

Body Application

  • White Oudh attar on both palms; rub together; place over Heart Chakra.
  • Optional: dust the body lightly with Chandan powder.
Avarans 4 & 5 — Sarva-Saubhagya Dayaka & Sarvartha-Sadhaka ⌄
4th & 5th Avarans
Sarva-Saubhagya Dayaka & Sarvartha-Sadhaka
Duration — 40-day rule Complete 40 days total. Not necessarily continuous. Patterns: 3 × 14 days, 6 × 7 days, or 4 × 11 days.

Two-Part Process

  1. First: Mantra Jaap (mansik) with full concentration.
  2. Then: Meditate on Lalita Sahasranama (listen to audio or mental recitation).

Important: This time always do Mantra Jaap first, then Lalita Sahasranama meditation.

Diya Instructions

  • Use Olive Oil in the lamp.
  • Fragrance: Lavender or Rose attar / perfume / aroma oil (if available).

Yantra Instructions

  • Keep all old yantras safely stored in a cupboard.
  • Make two copies of the current yantra — one for Tratak, one on the floor with a diya on it.
Avarans 6 & 7 — Sarvarakṣakara & Sarva Rogahara Chakras ⌄
6th & 7th Avarans · Protection & Healing
Sarvarakṣakara & Sarva Rogahara
Objective Invoke Devi's protective and healing forces — purifying the aura, stabilizing prāṇa, awakening subtle immunity through fragrance, mantra and inner focus.

Main Sadhana Flow

  1. Tratak & meditation on Sri Yantra (30–45 min) — focus on 6th & 7th Avaran centres.
  2. Mansik Japa (Jñāna Mudrā) of Source Mantra — 30–60 minutes.
  3. Energy alignment with bhavana: "This light shields, heals, and renews every cell through Devi's grace."
  4. Closing — 5 minutes of silent gratitude.

Tilak Paste — Rakṣā Lehyam

  • Sandalwood — 1 pinch
  • Vetiver (Khus) — 1 pinch
  • Saffron — 1 pinch
  • Camphor (Bhimseni) — 1 grain
  • Rose water / Ganga jal — 1 drop

Two Diyas — Dīpa-Taila Dvaya

🪔
Rakṣā Dīpa
Til oil + sandalwood, khus, saffron, gold-leaf, camphor oil / Hina attar. Symbolizes protection.
🪔
Rogahara Dīpa
Pure cow ghee. Symbolizes Devi's healing & purifying aspect.

Offerings

  • One fresh fruit daily (banana, pomegranate, coconut).
  • Panchmeva: Cashew, Almonds, Raisins, Dates, Figs.
  • Panchamrit: Cow milk, curd, ghee, honey, sugar/mishri — return to nature (plant/tree) after the practice.
  • Flower: Hibiscus, Lotus, Rose, or any fresh flower.
Essence "Where fragrance, flame, and mantra unite — there Devi abides as protection, peace, and health."

If any listed ingredient is unavailable, you may skip it — intention and purity of practice matter more than completeness of materials.
Avarans 8 & 9 — Sarva-Siddhi-Pradāyaka & Sarvānandamaya (Final) ⌄
8th · Floor Yantra
Sarva-Siddhi-Pradāyaka
9th · Wall Yantra (Bindu)
Sarvānandamaya
The Last Two Chakras Practiced without audio, without Lalita Sahasranam — only lamp, yantra, mantra, and awareness.

Setup

  • Floor: Place Sarva-Siddhi-Pradāyaka Chakra. One diya at its centre. Plain til oil or ghee with just 1 edible gold leaf. Nothing else.
  • Wall: Place Sarvānandamaya Chakra at eye level, directly in front of where you sit.

Phase 1 — Flame Tratak

  • Sit facing the floor yantra's diya.
  • Gaze softly at the tip of the flame.
  • Only mansik jaap of Source Mantra — no audio, no spoken mantra, no visualization.
  • Remain steady 15–30 minutes or until the mind becomes calm.

Phase 2 — Bindu Tratak

  • Shift gaze to the centre (bindu) of the wall yantra.
  • Continue soft mansik jaap of Source Mantra.
  • Let the mantra naturally become subtle or silent. Do not force concentration.
  • Stay as long as comfortable.

Completion

  • Practice for 11 to 21 consecutive days.
  • After the cycle, collect all old yantras used.
  • Perform a Lalita Tripura Sundari Havan.
  • During the havan, burn the yantras as an offering — closure into Devi's hands.
After Completion Your Trilok Nagri access becomes active. For any future help, healing, protection, or specific result:
  • Place the complete Sri Yantra in front of you.
  • Light the same gold-leaf oil diya.
  • Sit in Tratak on the Sri Yantra; mansik jaap of Source Mantra.
  • Take a clear Sankalp (what you need, how many days / japa count).
  • After the Sankalp period, perform a Havan.
The work is then carried within your own Trilok Nagri, channelised through Kul Devta, Isht Devta, and Pitra Devta.
CHAPTER IV

Kālashtamī Sādhana

A night of transformation — observed on Kartika Krishna Ashtami — to dissolve unresolved karmas, pitra doṣa, and hidden blockages under Mahākālī's grace.

Purpose On this tithi the veils between past, present and future thin — Mahākālī's energy realigns timelines and destroys hidden blockages.
Timing & Direction ⌄
Kartika, Krishna Ashtami The eighth lunar day of the dark fortnight of Kartika — the tithi sacred to Mahākālī.
Best Sadhana Window After sunset, preferably between 8:00 PM – 11:00 PM
Duration 45 min – 2 hours depending on mala count
Direction Sit facing South — the direction of Kāla, Time & Death
Preparation ⌄
  1. Clean your space — remove all yantras, taweez, dhāgas, pitra/guru photos, and ritual objects.
  2. Oil Lamp: sesame (til) oil or mustard oil in a clay or brass diya. Light using burning camphor — not a matchstick or lighter. Let the flame burn continuously.
  3. Fragrance: Loban, Guggal, or regular incense.
  4. Seat: An āsan — avoid direct floor contact.
  5. Mood: Silence. No phone. No lights except the diya.
Mantra Practice ⌄
Primary Mantra
ॐ क्रीं कालिकायै नमः
Om Krīṁ Kālikāyai Namaḥ

Do 1 / 3 / 7 / 11 / 21 / 33 / 108 malas as per energy and devotion. Chant calmly & mentally — cuts cords of past karmas, dissolves timeline-based blockages.

Balancing Mantra (Triple Count)
ॐ ऐं ह्रीं क्लीं कालरात्र्यै नमः
Om Aiṁ Hrīṁ Klīṁ Kālarātryai Namaḥ

For each mala of the primary mantra, chant 3 malas of this. Neutralizes leftover karmic traces, removes devic or ancestral disturbances, stabilizes your energy field.

Silent Absorption (Kāl Dhyān) & Closing ⌄

Kāl Dhyān

After completing all mantras, sit silently for 11–21 minutes. Stay still; let the mantra energy settle naturally. No visualization, no effort — only awareness and silence.

Closing

  • Offer the remaining lamp oil (after cooling) at the base of a Peepal or Neem tree next morning.
  • Wash your mala with rose water or Ganga jal.
  • Avoid non-veg and salt till next sunrise.
Spiritual Note Kālashtamī is not a night of worship — it is a night of transformation. When you chant these mantras with surrender, you are not invoking destruction outside — you are clearing the karmic weight within. Mahākālī frees you from time itself. Kālarātrī resets what destiny once locked.
Important Note This sadhana is not compulsory for everyone — only those who feel called. Contact your guide before performing it. Ensure your space has no Dhaga, Taweez, Yantra, Murti, Pitr, or Tantra-related objects, or new energies may unintentionally take their place.
CHAPTER V

Navratri Sadhana — Chamunda Mata

Direct connection with Chamunda Mata Mandir Shaktis through the Navarna Mantra — observed during the auspicious Sharad Navratri.

Navarna Mantra
ऐं ह्रीं क्लीं चामुण्डायै विच्चे
Aiṁ Hrīṁ Klīṁ Chāmuṇḍāyai Vichche
Sadhana Instructions ⌄
  • Daily Jaap: 33 malas in one sitting with an oil lamp burning.
  • Duration: Choose 9, 11, or 21 days (with Sankalp).
  • After Sankalp: Continue with 3 malas daily, or at least on Amavasya / Poornima / once a week — to maintain spiritual connection.
Who Can Do This ⌄
Suitable For
  • Advanced sadhaks already engaged in regular mantra sadhana for the past year.
  • New seekers who are disciplined and ready, provided they follow instructions properly.
Should Avoid Sankalp Sadhna
  • Beginners who haven't followed basic cleansing or mantra guidelines.
  • Those currently in the middle of 1.25 lakh mantra purashcharan.
  • Seekers under Pitra Doshas or Shakti Doshas.
Such people may still do daily jaap without Sankalp for blessings and gradual energy alignment.
CHAPTER VI

Diwali Sadhana Week — Guidance

Four pathways for the Diwali week — choose according to your spiritual stage and material aspirations.

Mahalakshmi Havan with Kamal Gatte Perform Mahalakshmi Havan during Labh Muhurat using Kamal Gatte (lotus seeds).

Rule: Use equal quantity of Kamal Gatta as your Havan Samagri. Example — if you prepare 1 kg Havan Samagri, add 1 kg Kamal Gatta.
Idol Placement Avoid keeping idols during Diwali Puja if daily rituals will not be continued after Diwali. Once installed, idols require daily care, lighting, and offering. Those who can maintain rituals daily may proceed with the full traditional Puja.
For Siddhi Sadhaks (not actively recommended) Those walking the Siddhi or Mantra Sadhana path may chant the Navarn Mantra.

Take a proper Sankalp before beginning. Example: "I will complete 11,000 / 21,000 Navarn Mantra Jaap during Diwali week." You may include a Havan using the same mantra for deeper Shakti activation.
For Material Benefit Seekers
  • Mahalakshmi Mantra Havan for wealth and prosperity.
  • Ganesha Mantra Havan for removing blockages and ensuring success.

Muslims may do Surah Fatiha and Surah Ikhlas.
